# Decision Rights

Decision rights clarify who can make which data decisions and how conflicts are resolved.

## Common decision areas

| Decision | Recommended owner | Escalation path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business definition | Data owner | Domain working group, then governance council |
| Metric calculation | Metric owner or data owner | Governance council |
| Data quality threshold | Data owner with steward input | Governance council for cross-domain impact |
| Access approval | Data owner or delegated approver | Risk/security/privacy partner for sensitive data |
| Source-of-record designation | Domain owner | Executive steering for enterprise conflicts |
| Exception approval | Control owner | Governance council or executive sponsor |

## Decision log minimum fields

- Decision statement.
- Decision owner.
- Alternatives considered.
- Rationale.
- Effective date.
- Impacted data assets, reports, products, or systems.
- Review date or expiration date.
