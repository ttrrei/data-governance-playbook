# Governance Forum

A governance forum is a recurring decision and escalation mechanism for cross-domain data topics.

## Purpose

- Approve or revise governance standards.
- Resolve definition, ownership, quality, and access conflicts.
- Prioritize remediation for critical issues.
- Review metrics, risks, exceptions, and adoption.
- Escalate unresolved decisions to executive sponsors.

## Suggested cadence

| Forum | Cadence | Participants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domain working group | Weekly or biweekly | Owners, stewards, custodians, consumers |
| Enterprise governance council | Monthly | Domain owners, governance lead, risk/security/privacy partners |
| Executive steering | Quarterly | Executive sponsor, senior data and business leaders |

## Effective forum rules

- Bring decisions, not status updates only.
- Use clear decision owners and due dates.
- Document outcomes, rationale, and follow-up actions.
- Track repeated issues as systemic improvement opportunities.
