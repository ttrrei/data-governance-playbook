# Issue Management

Governance issue management provides a repeatable way to identify, prioritize, resolve, and learn from data problems.

## Issue types

- Data quality defects.
- Conflicting definitions or metric logic.
- Missing or unclear ownership.
- Access delays or inappropriate access.
- Metadata gaps or stale catalog entries.
- Lineage uncertainty.
- Policy exceptions or control failures.

## Triage fields

- Issue summary and affected data asset.
- Business impact and affected stakeholders.
- Severity and urgency.
- Root-cause category.
- Owner and remediation team.
- Target resolution date.
- Workaround, if any.
- Closure evidence and prevention action.

## Severity guide

| Severity | Description | Expected response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Material regulatory, financial, customer, safety, or executive-decision impact | Immediate escalation and active remediation |
| High | Significant business process, reporting, or AI-use impact | Prioritized remediation with forum visibility |
| Medium | Localized impact or manageable workaround | Planned remediation |
| Low | Documentation, usability, or minor metadata issue | Backlog and batch resolution |
