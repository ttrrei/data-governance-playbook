# Roles and Responsibilities

## Core roles

| Role | Primary responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Executive sponsor | Sets mandate, removes blockers, funds priority work, and reinforces adoption. |
| Data governance lead | Runs the governance operating model, standards, roadmap, and performance reporting. |
| Data owner | Owns business decisions for a domain, dataset, metric, or data product. |
| Data steward | Maintains definitions, metadata, quality rules, and issue triage. |
| Technical custodian | Operates platforms, pipelines, access controls, and technical metadata. |
| Risk, privacy, or security partner | Advises on obligations, controls, exceptions, and evidence. |
| Data consumer | Uses data responsibly and reports defects, ambiguity, and access friction. |

## Responsibility design principles

- Put accountability close to the business process and decision.
- Separate business accountability from technical custody.
- Give stewards explicit capacity and measurable expectations.
- Make cross-domain decisions visible and time-bound.
