# General Enterprise Data Governance MVP

## Goal

Create the minimum operating foundation required to make priority enterprise data discoverable, understood, owned, trusted, and appropriately controlled.

## Scope

Start with 1-3 high-value data domains or cross-functional datasets that support executive reporting, regulatory obligations, or high-impact business processes.

## MVP capabilities

1. **Data ownership**: Name accountable business owners and operational stewards for each priority data area.
2. **Business glossary**: Define critical terms, metrics, and calculation rules used in decisions.
3. **Data catalog**: Register priority datasets with descriptions, owners, classifications, and usage context.
4. **Data quality controls**: Define a small set of measurable quality rules for critical data elements.
5. **Access control baseline**: Document who can access priority data, why they need it, and what approval path applies.
6. **Issue management**: Create a triage process for quality, definition, access, and ownership issues.
7. **Governance forum**: Establish a lightweight forum that resolves cross-domain decisions and escalations.

## 90-day rollout

| Phase | Duration | Outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | Weeks 1-2 | Select domains, stakeholders, priority decisions, and critical data elements. |
| Define | Weeks 3-5 | Confirm owners, glossary entries, classifications, and quality expectations. |
| Implement | Weeks 6-10 | Publish catalog entries, quality rules, access baseline, and issue workflow. |
| Operate | Weeks 11-13 | Run governance forum, track metrics, resolve issues, and refine standards. |

## Success metrics

- Priority datasets have named owners and stewards.
- Critical terms and metrics have approved definitions.
- Critical data elements have documented quality rules and issue owners.
- Access requests follow a visible approval process.
- Governance issues are triaged with service-level expectations.

## MVP exit criteria

The MVP is complete when priority data assets are documented, owners actively participate in governance routines, issues are tracked to resolution, and teams can demonstrate improved trust or reduced friction in at least one important business process.
