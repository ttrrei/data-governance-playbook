# AI-Ready Data Governance MVP

## Goal

Create the minimum governance foundation needed to support safe, reliable, and explainable analytics, machine learning, and generative AI use cases.

## Scope

Start with AI-relevant data products, model features, knowledge bases, retrieval corpora, or analytical datasets that influence automated recommendations, customer experiences, compliance reporting, or operational decisions.

## MVP capabilities

1. **AI data inventory**: Identify datasets used for model training, evaluation, prompting, retrieval, fine-tuning, and monitoring.
2. **Data classification**: Tag sensitive, regulated, confidential, licensed, and externally sourced data.
3. **Provenance and lineage**: Document source systems, transformations, enrichment steps, and downstream AI use cases.
4. **Quality and fitness checks**: Define completeness, accuracy, timeliness, representativeness, bias, and drift checks where relevant.
5. **Usage constraints**: Record allowed uses, prohibited uses, retention rules, consent constraints, and licensing restrictions.
6. **Human accountability**: Assign owners for data approval, model-use approval, and incident response.
7. **Monitoring and issue response**: Track data incidents, access exceptions, quality drift, and inappropriate reuse.

## 90-day rollout

| Phase | Duration | Outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identify | Weeks 1-2 | Select AI use cases and map the data assets they depend on. |
| Assess | Weeks 3-5 | Classify data, review provenance, define risks, and document usage constraints. |
| Control | Weeks 6-10 | Implement access, quality, lineage, and approval controls for priority assets. |
| Monitor | Weeks 11-13 | Establish metrics, issue workflow, and periodic governance review for AI data. |

## AI-ready evidence checklist

- Data source, owner, steward, and approved use are documented.
- Sensitive and restricted data is classified and access-controlled.
- Lineage is sufficient to explain how data reaches the AI use case.
- Quality rules are linked to model or application risk.
- Exceptions and incidents have accountable owners and response paths.

## MVP exit criteria

The MVP is complete when priority AI data assets have documented provenance, usage constraints, quality controls, ownership, and monitoring routines that can be reviewed before and after AI deployment.
