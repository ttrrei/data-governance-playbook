# Data Lineage

Data lineage describes where data comes from, how it changes, and where it is consumed.

## Levels of lineage

- **Business lineage**: High-level flow between processes, domains, reports, and products.
- **Technical lineage**: Tables, columns, pipelines, transformations, jobs, and dependencies.
- **Control lineage**: Where validations, approvals, masking, and retention controls are applied.

## Why lineage matters

Lineage supports impact analysis, incident response, regulatory evidence, model explainability, migration planning, and trust in analytics.

## Minimum lineage questions

- What is the original source of this data?
- What transformations or enrichment steps occur?
- Which systems, reports, data products, models, or teams consume it?
- Who owns each major step in the flow?
- Which controls apply along the path?
