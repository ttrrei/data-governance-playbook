# Business Glossary

A business glossary defines the terms, metrics, classifications, and business concepts that teams use when discussing and interpreting data.

## What to include

- Term name and approved definition.
- Business owner and steward.
- Synonyms, acronyms, and deprecated terms.
- Calculation rules for metrics.
- Related data elements, reports, products, and policies.
- Approval status and version history.

## Governance practices

- Prioritize terms used in executive reporting, regulatory processes, core operations, and AI features.
- Resolve conflicting definitions through decision rights and forums.
- Link glossary terms to catalog assets and quality rules.
- Review definitions when processes, products, regulations, or source systems change.
