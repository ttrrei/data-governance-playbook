# Data Ownership

Data ownership assigns accountability for data meaning, quality expectations, access decisions, lifecycle rules, and issue resolution.

## Ownership roles

- **Data owner**: Accountable business leader who makes priority and policy decisions for a domain or critical dataset.
- **Data steward**: Operational role that maintains definitions, rules, metadata, and issue triage.
- **Technical custodian**: Platform or engineering role that manages storage, pipelines, controls, and technical reliability.
- **Data consumer**: Person or team that uses data and provides feedback on fitness, quality, and usability.

## Good ownership looks like

- Owners have authority to make trade-offs and resolve conflicts.
- Stewards have time, tools, and process support.
- Technical custodians know which business expectations their systems must satisfy.
- Consumers know where to ask questions and report issues.
