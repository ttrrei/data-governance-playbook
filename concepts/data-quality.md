# Data Quality

Data quality describes whether data is fit for its intended business, analytical, operational, or AI use.

## Common dimensions

- **Accuracy**: Data correctly represents the real-world object or event.
- **Completeness**: Required values are present.
- **Timeliness**: Data is available and refreshed when needed.
- **Consistency**: Values align across systems and business rules.
- **Validity**: Values follow accepted formats, domains, and constraints.
- **Uniqueness**: Records are not duplicated beyond an accepted threshold.

## Governance practices

- Define critical data elements and the decisions they support.
- Translate business expectations into measurable rules.
- Assign owners for rules, thresholds, exceptions, and remediation.
- Monitor quality trends and connect issues to business impact.
- Use root-cause analysis to prevent repeated issues.

## Minimum artifact

Each critical data element should have a definition, system of record, quality rule, threshold, owner, monitoring frequency, and issue path.
